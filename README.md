# Customer Segmentation and Retention Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

A customer segmentation and retention analytics project that identifies high-value and at-risk customers using **RFM analysis, K-Means clustering, and cohort/retention analysis** on retail transaction data.

The project combines **Python, Pandas, SQL/MySQL, Scikit-learn, and Tableau** to segment customers into actionable groups, analyze purchasing behavior and retention patterns, quantify revenue contribution and revenue at risk, and translate findings into targeted retention recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Identify high-value customers and customers showing signs of reduced engagement.
* Segment customers based on purchasing behavior.
* Understand customer purchase frequency and monetary contribution.
* Analyze customer retention across different acquisition cohorts.
* Identify revenue associated with at-risk and dormant customer segments.
* Provide data-driven recommendations for customer retention and engagement.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The transaction data was cleaned before analysis by:

* Removing transactions with missing Customer IDs.
* Removing duplicate records.
* Converting InvoiceDate into datetime format.
* Removing cancellation/return transactions using negative quantities.
* Removing zero-price transactions.
* Creating a `Revenue` metric using:

```text
Revenue = Quantity × Price
```

After cleaning, the dataset contained approximately **779K valid transaction records** and **5,878 customers** for the RFM analysis.

---

## 📐 RFM Analysis

RFM analysis was used to evaluate customer purchasing behavior using three metrics:

| Metric    | Meaning                                           |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Recency   | Number of days since the customer's last purchase |
| Frequency | Number of unique invoices/purchases               |
| Monetary  | Total revenue generated by the customer           |

The RFM variables were log-transformed and standardized before applying K-Means clustering to reduce the impact of highly skewed customer values.

---

## 🤖 Customer Segmentation

K-Means clustering was used to group customers based on their RFM behavior.

Five business-oriented customer segments were created:

| Segment                    | Customers | Customer % | Revenue % | Business Interpretation                                       |
| -------------------------- | --------: | ---------: | --------: | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Champions                  |       458 |      7.79% |    54.88% | Highly active and high-value customers                        |
| Loyal Customers            |     1,294 |     22.01% |    27.36% | Consistent customers with strong value                        |
| At-Risk Customers          |     1,363 |     23.19% |    10.87% | Previously valuable customers with declining activity         |
| New / Developing Customers |     1,143 |     19.45% |     4.49% | Recently acquired customers with developing purchase behavior |
| Dormant Customers          |     1,620 |     27.56% |     2.40% | Customers with long periods since their last purchase         |

> **Note:** Five clusters were selected as a business-oriented segmentation choice to create more actionable customer groups. The clustering evaluation was also reviewed using silhouette scores.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### 1. Champions generate a large share of revenue

Champions represent only **7.79% of customers** but contribute approximately **54.88% of total revenue**.

This highlights the importance of protecting and retaining high-value customers.

### 2. At-Risk customers represent an important retention opportunity

At-Risk customers account for approximately **23.19% of customers** and contribute around **10.87% of revenue**.

Targeted reactivation campaigns can help recover potentially valuable customers.

### 3. Dormant customers form the largest customer segment

Dormant customers represent approximately **27.56% of customers**, but contribute only around **2.40% of revenue**.

This suggests that broad win-back campaigns may not be equally effective for every dormant customer and should be prioritized based on customer value.

### 4. Loyal customers provide a strong growth opportunity

Loyal customers represent approximately **22.01% of customers** and contribute **27.36% of revenue**.

Encouraging loyal customers to increase purchase frequency and spending can help move them toward the Champions segment.

## 🔎 Cohort & Retention Analysis

Cohort analysis was performed by grouping customers according to the month of their first purchase.

The analysis tracks customer retention across subsequent months after their first purchase.

The resulting retention matrix was visualized as a **cohort retention heatmap in Tableau**, helping identify how customer retention changes over time across different customer cohorts.
